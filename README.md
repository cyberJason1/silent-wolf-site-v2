# 沉默之狼 · Silent Wolf
> 眼睛会骗人。结构不会。

**马来西亚华文独中｜写实校园足球 · 少年悬疑故事**

雨后积水的露天足球场，热带棕榈与老旧看台之间。
17岁中场陈知白拥有**结构视角**——推演赛场1‑3秒多种跑动概率，并非预知未来。

这份天赋带着记忆碎片化的代价，一部分由哥哥陈知夜清醒且自愿承担。
误解把他叫作「影子」；历经球场的挣扎与羁绊，他终将成为「天狼」。

匿名纸条、胶片相机、暗处的观察者与遥远监视的神秘组织。
没有热血爽文，只有少年的孤独、亲情、遗憾与缓慢的自我接纳。

A realistic sports‑mystery story set in a Malaysian Chinese independent high‑school.

Seventeen‑year‑old midfielder Chen Zhibai wields **Structural Vision**: he can simulate 1‑3‑second probabilistic movement paths on the football pitch, not true precognition.

This gift comes with a heavy toll. Part of its cost is voluntarily borne by his older brother Chen Zhiye.
Mislabeled as "the Shadow" by teammates, he grows to become "the Sirius".

Anonymous hand‑written notes, vintage film camera, hidden observers and a distant monitoring secret organization.
No over‑the‑top shonen triumph. Only teenage solitude, family bonds, regret and slow self‑acceptance.
